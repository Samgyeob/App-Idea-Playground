# Campus Helper

## Overview
Campus Helper is a practical Android app concept designed to help college students quickly access essential campus-related information in one place.  
The app focuses on reducing friction for everyday tasks rather than replacing official school systems.

## Problem
Students often rely on multiple websites or apps for basic campus information such as:
- Office hours
- Building locations
- Academic calendar dates
- Important links

This scattered experience makes simple tasks unnecessarily time-consuming.

## Target Users
- Community college students
- Transfer students
- First-year university students

## Core Features
- Quick-access links to commonly used campus resources
- Simple campus map with key buildings highlighted
- Academic calendar reminders (add-to-calendar support)
- Notes section for storing advisor instructions or deadlines

## Technical Focus
- Clean, list-based Android UI
- External link handling
- Basic local data persistence
- Optional calendar and notification integration

## Future Improvements
- Customizable campus profiles
- Offline access to saved information
- Push notifications for important dates
- Widget support for quick access

## Status
Idea and documentation stage.  
Designed as a realistic, student-focused utility app.
